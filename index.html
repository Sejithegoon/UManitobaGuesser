How to play

1. Study the photo

Each round shows a real photo taken somewhere on the University of Manitoba campus. Use the magnifier to zoom in on signs, doors and landmarks.

2. Drop your pin

Click or tap the map where you think the photo was taken. You can move your pin as many times as you like before you guess.

3. Beat the clock

Easy gives you 30 seconds, Medium 20 and Hard just 8. The closer your pin, the more points you earn, up to 5,000 per round.

UManitoba Guesser is a free, student-made game. Finish a game to see your rank, compare your personal best, and share your score with friends.
