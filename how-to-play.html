How to Play

The complete guide to UManitoba Guesser

UManitoba Guesser is a free photo-guessing game about the University of Manitoba. Each round shows you a real photo taken somewhere on campus, and your job is to find that spot on a map. The closer your guess, the more points you score. It's a quick way to learn the campus if you're new, and a real test if you've spent years walking between buildings.

The goal

Score as many points as you can by placing your pin as close as possible to where each photo was taken. A perfect game of 5 rounds is worth 25,000 points.

Where the photos are from

Every location in the game is on the University of Manitoba's Fort Garry campus in south Winnipeg. Photos come from inside and around buildings across campus, including University Centre, the Allen, Armes, Isbister and Parker buildings, the Duff Roblin Building, Drake Centre, Pembina Hall, Taché Hall, University College, St. John's College and St. Paul's College, the Education and Engineering buildings, and the athletic facilities. Some photos show famous landmarks; others show a hallway, a staircase or a doorway you've probably walked past without noticing.

New locations are added regularly, so the game changes as you play it more.

How a round works

  1. Study the photo. Look for clues: signs, room numbers, building materials, windows, the shape of a hallway, or what you can see outside.
  2. Drop your pin. Click or tap the map where you think the photo was taken. You can move your pin as many times as you like.
  3. Guess. Press Guess (or the Enter key) to lock in your answer before the timer runs out.
  4. See the result. The map shows the real location, a line to your guess, how far off you were, and the points you earned.
  5. Next round. Press Next Round to keep going. After the last round you'll see your final score and rank.

If the timer runs out before you guess, the round scores 0 points.

Difficulty and rounds

Pick a difficulty and a number of rounds on the home page before you start. Difficulty only changes how much time you get per round:

  • Easy: 30 seconds per round
  • Medium: 20 seconds per round
  • Hard: 8 seconds per round

You can play 5, 10 or 20 rounds, or choose Unlimited and keep going until you press End Game. Each combination of difficulty and rounds keeps its own personal best score on your device.

Scoring

Each round is worth up to 5,000 points. Any guess within 10 metres of the real spot gets the full 5,000. After that, points drop smoothly the further away you are, so being close matters much more than being roughly right:

  • 30 m away: about 4,500 points
  • 100 m away: about 3,200 points
  • 200 m away: about 1,900 points
  • 400 m away: about 700 points
  • 1 km or more away: almost nothing

After each guess you'll see a quick rating: Bullseye (under 30 m), Great guess (under 200 m), Not bad (under 600 m) or Way off.

Ranks

At the end of a game you get a rank based on the share of possible points you earned:

  • Campus Legend: 90% or more
  • Seasoned Senior: 70% or more
  • Dean's List: 45% or more
  • First-Year Fresh: 20% or more
  • Lost in the Tunnels: under 20%

You can share your result with friends as a row of coloured squares, one per round, without giving away the locations.

Controls

  • Place a pin: click or tap the map.
  • Move around the map: drag to pan; scroll, pinch or use the + and − buttons to zoom.
  • Zoom into the photo: on a computer, hover over the photo to use the magnifier. On any device, open the full-size viewer to zoom with the scroll wheel or a pinch, drag to look around, and double-tap to zoom in. On phones, tapping the photo opens the viewer.
  • Keyboard: Enter guesses, moves to the next round and closes the rules. In the photo viewer, + and − zoom and Escape closes it.
  • Sound: use the speaker button in the top bar to mute or unmute.

Tips for a better score

  • Read every sign. Room numbers, department names and plaques often give the building away.
  • Look at the materials. Older buildings and newer ones look very different: brick and stone versus glass and concrete.
  • Use the windows. If you can see outside, look for nearby buildings, parking lots or green space.
  • Think about the tunnels. Many Fort Garry buildings are linked by tunnels and walkways. A hallway photo might be at the edge of a building, close to a connection.
  • Zoom the map in before you guess. Being off by one building can cost hundreds of points, so place your pin on the exact wing or entrance.
  • Start on Easy. The extra time lets you learn the campus. Switch to Hard once you recognize places quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account, no sign-up and nothing to buy. Ads help cover the cost of running the site.

Do I need to be a U of M student?

No. Anyone can play, but people who know the Fort Garry campus will have an advantage.

Does my score get saved?

Your personal best for each mode is saved in your own browser. There's no public leaderboard, and nothing is sent to a server.

I found a photo in the wrong place. How do I report it?

Email umanitobaguesser@gmail.com or message us on Discord with the location and what looks wrong.
