Our Mission 🦬

UManitoba Guesser was created by students, for students. Our goal is to help Bisons explore the corners of the University of Manitoba's Fort Garry campus while testing how well they really know it.

The Game

Whether you're a first-year student trying to find Tier or a seasoned senior who knows every shortcut in the tunnels, this game is for you.

Why we built it

Every September, thousands of new students try to find their first classes on a campus with dozens of buildings, connecting tunnels and hallways that all start to look the same. Upper-year students know the shortcuts, but there was never a fun way to learn them. UManitoba Guesser turns that into a game: you see a real photo from somewhere on campus and try to pin it on the map.

It's inspired by geography guessing games, but focused on a single place we all know. Some photos are easy, like a famous building front. Others are a hallway, a stairwell or a lounge that only regulars will recognize.

The campus

The University of Manitoba, founded in 1877, was Western Canada's first university. Its main campus, Fort Garry, sits in south Winnipeg inside a bend of the Red River. It's home to most of the university's faculties, the student union at University Centre, the libraries, residences such as Taché Hall and Pembina Hall, the affiliated colleges (including St. John's, St. Paul's and University College) and the athletic facilities.

Many Fort Garry buildings are connected by underground tunnels and enclosed walkways, which get a lot of use during Winnipeg winters. That's part of what makes the game tricky: two hallways in different buildings can be a short walk apart underground.

The university's Bannatyne campus is in downtown Winnipeg beside the Health Sciences Centre and is the hub for health sciences programs. All current game locations are on the Fort Garry campus.

How the site works

UManitoba Guesser runs entirely in your browser. The map uses OpenStreetMap through the open-source Leaflet library, and scoring is based on the real distance between your pin and the spot where the photo was taken. We add new locations regularly. There are no accounts, and your best scores are saved only on your own device.

We also built a free Class Calendar tool, because UM doesn't offer a way to export your timetable. Search for your classes and download one file that adds them to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official University of Manitoba website?

No. UManitoba Guesser is an independent, student-made project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Manitoba.

Does it cost anything?

No. The game and the Class Calendar are free and don't need an account. Ads on some pages help pay for the domain and hosting.

Which campus is in the game?

Right now, every location is on the Fort Garry campus.

Can I suggest a location or report a mistake?

Yes. Email umanitobaguesser@gmail.com or message us on Discord. If a pin looks like it's in the wrong spot, tell us which photo it was.

Can I help build it?

We're always happy to hear from students who want to help with code, photos or ideas. Join the Discord to get involved.

Is my data collected?

We don't collect personal information. See the Privacy Policy for what's stored on your device and how advertising works.
