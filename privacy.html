Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

UManitoba Guesser (umanitobaguesser.ca) is a free, student-made game. This page explains what information is used when you visit the site.

What we collect

You don't need an account to play, and we never ask for your name. If you create an account, we use your email address to sign you in.

Guest game sessions

To keep scores fair, the game checks your guesses on our server instead of in your browser. When you start playing:

  • A guest session is created. Your browser gets a random guest ID and a session token, saved in local storage so you stay the same guest on your next visit. It isn't linked to your name, email or any account.
  • Your rounds are stored on our server under that random ID: the point you picked on the map, how far off it was, your score, the difficulty and number of rounds, and when each round started and was answered.
  • Where: our database is hosted by Supabase in its Canada (Central) region. Like any web service, Supabase also logs technical details of each request, such as your IP address, to run and protect the service.
  • How long: guest data is deleted automatically once a guest hasn't started a game for 30 days (checked daily). Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the session token, and the records on our server can then no longer be linked to you.

Bot check

The first time your browser starts a guest session, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person and not a bot. It's usually invisible. Cloudflare processes technical information from your browser to do this, and uses it only to detect bots.

Accounts (optional)

  • Email: we sign you in by emailing a one-time code, so there's no password. Your email is stored by our authentication provider, Supabase, and is never shown to other players.
  • Username: the name you choose is public. It appears on the leaderboard next to your best ranked scores.
  • Games: your finished games, scores and best scores are linked to your account. When you create a new account in a browser where you've been playing as a guest, your guest games stay with your new account. If you sign in to an account you already had, your guest games are deleted with other guest data after 30 days.
  • University verification: to join the ranked leaderboard, you can verify a @myumanitoba.ca or @umanitoba.ca email. We email that address a one-time code. We don't store the address: we keep only a one-way scrambled fingerprint of it (a keyed hash), so the same university email can't verify two accounts, plus the domain and the date you verified.
  • Email delivery: codes are sent by our email provider, Resend, which processes the recipient address to deliver the message and keeps delivery logs under its privacy policy.
  • Deleting your account: on your account page you can delete your account at any time after confirming with a code. Your account, username, games, scores and verification are deleted immediately. We keep only your account's random ID and the deletion date for 35 days, so the deletion can be re-applied if we ever restore a backup.

Data stored on your device

The site saves a few small preferences in your browser's local storage so the game works the way you left it:

  • Your light or dark theme choice
  • Whether sound is muted
  • Your personal best score for each game mode
  • The classes and calendar settings you choose on the Class Calendar page
  • Your guest session token (see above), which is sent only to our game server

Apart from the guest session token, this data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which helps cover the cost of running the site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices or, in Canada, youradchoices.ca.
  • If you opt out, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized based on your browsing.

In regions where consent is legally required, such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google only shows personalized ads to visitors who have given consent. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads are not shown on the game screen itself.

Third-party services

To work, the site loads resources from third parties. Your browser sends them standard request information such as your IP address when it does so:

  • OpenStreetMap, for the map tiles
  • unpkg.com, for the Leaflet map library
  • jsDelivr, for the Supabase library that talks to our game server
  • Supabase, which hosts our game server, accounts and database (Canada)
  • Resend, which sends sign-in and verification emails
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, for the bot check
  • Font Awesome and Google Fonts, for icons and fonts
  • Google AdSense, for advertising

Links to Discord and Instagram take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The site is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Guest sessions aren't linked to names or contact details, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
